# The APHL film on eleven_v3, loudness normalized

Rebuilt 2026-09-17 after review: "still a little inconsistent, some parts more
enunciated and louder than others."
Deliverables: `~/.agi/renders/4f9166a4-.../film_v3/`. Runtime 213.121354 s.

## The loudness complaint was measurable, and is fixed

| | quietest | loudest | spread | stdev |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| previous build, turbo | -29.4 | -21.2 | **8.2 LU** | 2.19 |
| raw v3, before normalizing | -22.2 | -18.7 | 3.4 LU | 0.84 |
| **v3, normalized to -20 LUFS** | **-21.6** | **-20.3** | **1.3 LU** | **0.30** |

Three LU is a clearly audible step and six reads as roughly twice as loud, so an
8.2 LU spread across 31 lines is exactly what "some parts louder than others"
sounds like. Two-pass EBU R128 normalization at I=-20, TP=-1.5, LRA=11, applied
per line before assembly.

**v3 helped on its own**, arriving at 3.4 LU where turbo gave 8.2. Normalization
took it the rest of the way.

## What v3 does and does not support

Probed rather than assumed.

| parameter | result |
|---|---|
| `model_id: eleven_v3` | works |
| `seed` | **honoured and reproducible**, 3.440 s twice on the same seed |
| `stability`, `similarity_boost` | accepted |
| **`speed`** | **silently ignored**. 3.76 s at 1.0, 0.9, 0.8 and 0.7, identical to the millisecond |

That last one is the consequence to know about. `eleven_turbo_v2_5` at speed 0.9
gave 157.5 wpm, which is the pace this film was fitted to. **v3 has no rate
control and reads at about 177 wpm.**

## The cost, stated plainly

Faster delivery against a fixed picture means more silence.

| act | silence, turbo build | silence, v3 build |
|---|---|---|
| Act 1 | 16.1% | 24.7% |
| Act 2 | 19.5% | 23.1% |
| Act 3 | 24.3% | 30.8% |
| Act 4 | 18.1% | 27.2% |
| Act 5 | 24.5% | 29.7% |

All inside the 10 to 35% the validator permits, but above the 15 to 20% target,
and `assemble_voice` flags acts 4 and 5 explicitly: "OVER 25%. Set wpm to about
176 in the spec and write more, not shorter."

**That advice is right and I have not acted on it.** Bringing every act back into
band means adding roughly 10 to 15 spoken words per act, about 60 words across the
film, written to the beats that already exist. It is authoring, not re-fitting,
and it should be a decision rather than something I do silently.

The film is watchable as it stands. The gaps are longer, not wrong.

## What I cannot check

Whether the enunciation variation is gone. I measured loudness and I measured
rate; **timbre and emphasis I cannot hear.** If v3 still reads unevenly, the
remaining levers are a different voice, or v3's audio-tag markup, which lets a
line carry an explicit delivery instruction. Both are worth trying before
concluding the voice is wrong.
