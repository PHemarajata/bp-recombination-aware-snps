Act 4 is rebuilt, narrated and accepted, so these four are what stand between the
APHL film and a finished cut. Their instructions were spread across three
documents. This is all of it, per act, in the order to do it.

Do all of an act's changes in one render. Every item below moves beats, so an act
re-rendered twice gets audited twice and timed twice.

### Act 3 The Window, 46.2 s. The one that needs real work.

| item | detail |
|---|---|
| trim | **9.1 s**, to 37.1 s. Largest still stretch is 10.9 s. |
| frame audit | **11 overlaps**, several above 85% |
| em dashes | 4, `act3_body.py` lines 39, 65, 90, 135 |

**The collision is larger and longer than the earlier note recorded.** That note
described four text elements colliding from roughly 18 s to 42 s. The audit finds
**eleven overlaps spanning 18.0 s to 45.5 s**, in a 46.2 s act, with shares of 92,
89, 88, 88, 84, 73 and 67 percent. It is not a stretch in the middle of the act,
it is most of the act, and it runs to the last second.

**The cause is visible in a frame at 44 s.** The two edge labels, "too uniform:
nothing to detect" on the left and "too diverse: cannot align" on the right, sit at
the same vertical position as the dot swarm. The out-of-window circles are drawn
straight through both. Left and right, not just left.

That makes it a layout decision rather than a nudge: **the labels and the dots
need different bands.** Either lift the labels clear of the swarm, or drop the
swarm clear of the labels. Do not solve it by shortening the label text, because
the words carry the act's teaching point and the act is the mechanism the whole
film rests on.

This act also carries the worst of both problems at once, so it is the one to do
first. If its trim and its layout fix fight each other, the layout wins and the
trim gives way.

### Act 5 The Ceiling, 46.6 s

| item | detail |
|---|---|
| trim | **9.5 s**, to 37.1 s. **Spread across two gaps**, see below. |
| frame audit | 1 `tight` at 5.5 to 20.0 s, inside the 3% safe margin. A judgement call, not a failure. |
| em dashes | 6, `act5_body.py` lines 17, 37, 38, 50, 64, 88 |
| **content fix** | line 50, see below |

**The trim equals the act's largest single gap exactly**, 9.5 s against a 9.5 s
stretch. Taking it all from one place removes that hold entirely. Split it across
the 9.50 s and 7.82 s stretches.

**Line 50 is a content fix wearing a punctuation fix's clothes.** It reads
`fails — barely above chance`. Country attribution is twenty two percent against a
twenty six percent baseline, so it is at or below chance, not above it.
**`fails: no better than chance`** is accurate and is also the stronger line.

Line 88 is a trailing dash on a two-part reveal. Restructure rather than
substitute.

## Verification, per act

1. `audit_frames.py` at the render's own resolution. **`clipped` is a hard
   failure. Act 3 must come back with no overlap above about 40%.**
2. `map_beats.py`. Every trim moves every subsequent beat, so the existing beat
   maps are void on re-render.
3. Grep the built scene for an em dash. Expect zero.
4. Act 5 only: confirm `no better than chance` replaced `barely above chance`.

Then narration, which can be written here: the voice is measured and the
credential is present on the Mac even though it is absent in the render
environment. Act 4's narration is the worked example, in
`NARRATION_ACT4_2026-09-17.md`.
