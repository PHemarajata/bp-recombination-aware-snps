## C3Act1

4. Lay diversity along the axis, low on the left.

6. The window was measured before any of these numbers were looked at.

## C3Act2

1. Pool all eighty five units together and the median r over m is five point five one.
2. That number is not a result. It blends real measurements with detection failures.
3. So apply the window instead.
4. Forty seven units fall inside it, holding one thousand three hundred eighty eight genomes.
5. Their median is seven point seven zero.
6. Twelve units fall below the window, at one point three two.
7. Twenty six sit above the window, at two point one four.
8. Half of them fall between five point seven and nine point four.
9. Seven point seven zero inside.
10. Pooled outside it, thirty eight units, one point nine nine.
11. Same arithmetic. Two populations of units.
12. The contrast between seven point seven zero and one point nine nine is the finding.
13. Three classes. Only one of them is a measurement.

## C3Act3

1. Nothing new appears. The same picture, read the other way round.
2. A unit below the window is not a unit with little recombination.
3. It has too little diversity for the detector to find any.
4. So it returns a small number, and the small number means nothing.
5. A unit above the window has so much diversity that the estimate falls apart.
6. Both edges return a low number, for opposite reasons.
7. A low r over m is a detection failure.
8. Not a quiet genome.
9. Only the units inside the window are quiet or loud on purpose.
10. Every number outside the window is the ruler running out of range.
11. This is the single most important idea in the series.

## C3Act4Lead

1. The classes are not permanent.
2. A decision taken for good reasons elsewhere in the pipeline can move a unit.
3. It can leave the measurable set without anyone noticing.

## RecursiveSubdivision

1. This unit sat comfortably inside the working window.
2. The evidence it held two populations was clear.
3. So it was divided into three.
4. Every genome carries over. Nothing is lost.
5. Now place the parent and the children on the diversity scale.
6. Two of the three fall below the window.
7. One drops eighteen fold, from thirteen hundred ten down to seventy two.
8. For those two, no rate can be interpreted.
9. The division itself was correct.
10. The cost was invisible.

## C3Act5Lead

1. Remember that r over m is not measured directly. It is estimated on a tree.
2. So does the choice of tree builder change the answer?
3. Twelve units, three builders, run on exactly the same alignments.

## TreeBuilderPaired

1. Does the tree underneath the estimate change the number you get?
2. Anything below the line means less recombination than RAxML found on the same alignments.
3. The two maximum likelihood builders sit astride the line, seven of twelve below.
4. That is what chance looks like, not bias.
5. The fast distance builder sits below almost every time. That is systematic.

## C3Act7

1. So whose measurement is this?
2. Of the forty seven in-window units, thirty four contain isolates this project sequenced.
3. Light them up and they are spread across the measurable set.
4. Without them, ten of those thirty four would hold fewer than seven genomes.
5. Those ten would not exist as measurements at all.
6. Across all thirty four funded units, the median r over m is seven point seven four.
7. Against seven point seven zero for all in-window units.
8. Ten units depend on this project's isolates. The number itself does not.
