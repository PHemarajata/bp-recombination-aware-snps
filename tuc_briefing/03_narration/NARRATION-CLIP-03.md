## Act 3. The reversal

1. Nothing new appears now. The same picture, read the other way round.
2. This is the single most important idea in the series.
3. A unit below the window is not a unit with little recombination.
4. It has too little diversity for the detector to find any.
5. So it returns a small number, and the small number means nothing.
6. A unit above the window has so much diversity that the estimate falls apart.
7. Both edges return a low number, for opposite reasons.
8. A low r over m is a detection failure.
9. Not a quiet genome.
10. That is the reversal this whole series rests on.
