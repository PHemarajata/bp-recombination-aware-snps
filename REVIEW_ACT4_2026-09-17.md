## What the act does well

**Beat 2 answers the refusal clause.** The revision note warned that an
association between two variables is abstract to draw, and offered the builder an
exit if it could not be made concrete without inventing a picture of units passing
a test. It was made concrete instead: 119 project marks with 113 filled and six
left as outlines, so a viewer counts the exception, then one association read
against a labeled scale, "0 is separable, 1 is the same variable", with the
measured value at 0.857. No test, no pass, no count.

**The colors carry meaning rather than decoration.** Both project classes stay in
the geography channel and separate by fill against outline, rather than reaching
for the measured-signal teal to distinguish two facts about place. That is the
harder and the correct choice.

**The quantities match the claims.** Beat 1 is a composition claim and uses a
share. Beat 3 is a position claim and plots position against the window. Beat 2 is
an association and plots the association.

**The pivot ends the act where it was asked to.** "None of this says the genome is
uninformative. It says the country question is not the one this collection can
settle." That narrows the question rather than closing on the third limit, which
is what stops acts 3 to 5 reading as a list of things that did not work.

a defect, it is worth knowing the whole film does this:

|---|---|---|

| **new Act 4** | **9%** | 14.6 s |

At 9% active this is **the most active narrated act in the film**, and it improves
on the act it replaces in both measures. The hold-heavy style is house-wide, not a
property of this rebuild, and the trims in `ANIMO_APHL_TIGHTEN_2026-09-17.md`
address it where it is worst.

**The consequence is a narration constraint, not a picture fix.** At 110 words the
act carries roughly nine lines against six moments, so about three lines will have
no visual change to sit on. Those lines must **draw conclusions over a held frame,
never claim motion the viewer cannot see.** That is exactly the `7.09` failure in
miniature, and it is cheap to avoid while the narration is still unwritten.

**The pivot is a text-only frame on a 7.2 s tail.** Legitimate, since it is a
conclusion. When narration is timed, land the last line near the 43.0 s pivot beat
and let the tail be deliberate silence rather than stretching a line across it.
